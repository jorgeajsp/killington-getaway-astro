Killington isn't just a winter destination. When the snow melts and the trails dry out, the Beast of the East transforms into one of New England's premier mountain bike destinations — and if you haven't ridden here in summer, you're missing out on half the mountain's magic.

## The Bike Park Experience

Killington Bike Park opens each June and runs through October, offering over 40 miles of singletrack and lift-served downhill. Whether you're a first-time rider or a seasoned gravity enthusiast, there's something here for every skill level. The blue-rated runs are buttery smooth — perfect for building speed and confidence — while the black and double-black lines will test even the most experienced shredders.

The K-1 Gondola serves as the primary uplift, whisking riders to the summit where views of the Green Mountains stretch endlessly in every direction. From up top, you can drop into Juggernaut, a long flowing descent that weaves through the forest, or hit the jump lines and technical rock gardens if you're looking for a challenge.

## Best Trails for Beginners

New to mountain biking? Don't be intimidated. Killington's bike school offers lessons for all ages, and the green trails near the base area give you plenty of space to learn bike handling before committing to anything steep. Instructors are patient, knowledgeable, and genuinely passionate about getting more people hooked on the sport.

## Gear Up Before You Go

Rentals are available at the base lodge — everything from trail bikes to full-suspension enduro rigs. A full-face helmet is highly recommended for lift-served riding. Northern Ski Works and Surefoot on the access road also carry gear and can help with any last-minute needs.

## After the Ride

Post-ride, the Killington access road has no shortage of cold beer and hearty food. The Foundry at Summit Pond is a local favorite for outdoor dining with views of the pond. The Rivershed offers Nashville-inspired comfort food that hits just right after a full day on the bike.

Whether you're a Vermont local looking for a weekend mission or a destination traveler building a summer itinerary, Killington's bike park belongs on your list.
